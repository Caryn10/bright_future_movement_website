Membership

Welcome to the membership page of Bright Future Movement Club. Please read our Membership Terms and Conditions carefully before becoming a member.

Membership Terms and Conditions

  1. Every member must obtain a membership card.
  2. Every new member shall pay a one-time registration fee of GH¢150.00.
  3. Every new member must complete the required registration and police forms.
  4. Members shall pay monthly dues of GH¢20.00.
  5. Members shall contribute GH¢120.00 each towards weddings, engagements and funeral celebrations.
  6. Members shall contribute GH¢70.00 towards naming ceremonies and graduation celebrations.
  7. Every member must provide spouse, partner or next-of-kin details during registration.
  8. In the event of a member's death, the spouse or designated beneficiary shall receive GH¢3,000.00, subject to the club's regulations.
  9. Final funeral celebrations shall involve only parents and the nuclear family and attract a contribution of GH¢3,000.00.
  10. Family naming ceremonies and graduation celebrations shall attract a contribution of GH¢1,500.00.
  11. Upon the death of a member, the registered next of kin shall receive GH¢4,000.00.
  12. The club shall provide an emergency support fund of GH¢1,000.00.
  13. A member who resigns from the club shall pay a resignation fee of GH¢10,000.00.
  14. Members must notify the executives at least one month before a planned programme. Failure to do so may mean that the club makes the required payment but may not attend the event.
  15. Marriage support shall be provided only once per member.
  16. A maximum of two naming ceremony supports shall be provided per member.
  17. A maximum of two graduation supports shall be provided per member.
  18. Members must respect the executives. Misconduct may attract a penalty of GH¢200.00.
  19. If a member fails to pay dues for six consecutive months, any benefits due to the member may be deducted from the outstanding arrears.
  20. Every new member must purchase the official club T-shirt.
  21. The membership card fee is GH¢10.00.
  22. A member who receives no benefit after one year shall receive a bottle of wine as appreciation.
  23. Members must wear the official funeral cloth and approved head scarf (Duku) during funeral activities.
